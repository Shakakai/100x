# About Me

Hi there! I'm Todd Cullen, an AI Product Consultant based in Shoreline, Washington. With over two decades of experience in tech and engineering, I'm passionate about pushing the boundaries of AI and machine learning to create innovative product experiences.

I design and develop emotive voice assistants, AI-driven data pipelines, and build full-stack web & mobile applications. I'm an active contributor to the open-source community, with projects like MindMeld, TypedLLM, and TypedTemplate.

One of my proudest achievements was developing a chatbot to assess and treat suicidal ideation in emergency departments. It was a challenging but incredibly rewarding project helping thousands of Americans at the darkest hour of their lives.

Throughout my career, I've created metrics and forecasting systems for real estate operations, developed ad servers handling billions of requests monthly, and built the first iOS app for the enterprise market.

I hold a BA in Biology from Wake Forest University, with a focus on computational biology.

In my free time, I enjoy playing ice hockey in the Kraken Hockey League, road cycling around Seattle, and backpacking with my dog, Starbuck, in the Cascades.
