Leading product for an **agent-based simulation platform** that lets organizations ask how a population will respond to a policy, a price, an outbreak, or a market shock — and get answers grounded in simulation rather than extrapolation.

## What shipped

- An **AI-powered data scientist**: an agentic system that plans and executes end-to-end data science workloads across petabyte-scale datasets without manual orchestration
- **Populus**, a Python-native distributed simulation platform built on Ray, handling up to 10PB in a single workload and deployable into customer environments
- **Prism**, a JSON-based DSL that lets large language models author and maintain entire interactive analytical applications
- Synthetic population as a product: two fully synthesized U.S. populations with an enrichment marketplace

Delivered the platform's first seven figures of revenue and lead a team of 12 across product, engineering, and data science.
