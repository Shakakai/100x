Founded and led a **native advertising platform** that let publishers monetize websites and mobile applications, solving the monetization gap for user-generated content at social scale.

## By the numbers

- Sold into roughly half a dozen of the largest social platforms of the era — **Reddit, Imgur, and 9GAG** — representing 20B+ monthly impressions
- Engineered an architecture handling **10B+ requests and 100B+ events monthly**
- Delivered ad serving **30% faster than Google DFP**, improving ad effectiveness by 5–10%
- Selected **Haskell** for the core platform, trading a harder hiring market for a codebase that let a small team iterate quickly with minimal production defects

Sold the company to Chaotic Moon in 2013 and continued as operator through the earnout.
