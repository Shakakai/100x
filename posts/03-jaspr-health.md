Owned the technical and regulatory engineering work required for **FDA clearance** — the first cleared product in the company's history — for a digital therapeutic delivering rapid suicide risk assessment and brief intervention in emergency departments.

## The work

- Led a clinical conversational AI built on a **deterministic rules-based core** for clinical safety, augmented with GPT-3.5 for message categorization and analysis
- Managed a multidisciplinary team of 20+ engineers, researchers, designers, and clinical experts
- Validated the product through user research and clinical trials funded by **NIH and SAMHSA** grants
- Secured HIPAA and HITECH compliance by rebuilding access control, data integrity, transmission security, and traceability across the platform
- Instrumented product development against clinical outcomes including PHQ-9, length of stay, discharge disposition, and NPS
