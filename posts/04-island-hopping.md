Two weeks, two islands, one increasingly confident scooter driver. Bali was the base — Ubud's green chaos, the cliffs of Uluwatu — and Lombok was the escape hatch, a ferry ride away and a decade behind in the best way.

## Ubud, Slowly

Ubud gets described as overrun, and parts of it are, but it rewards early mornings. **Tegallalang's rice terraces** at 6am, before the heat and the crowds, with mist still in the palm trees. A cooking class where I learned that my knife skills are an international incident. The **Monkey Forest**, where a macaque stole a water bottle with the practiced efficiency of a pickpocket.

## Uluwatu and the Bukit

Down on the Bukit Peninsula the island turns dry and dramatic. Cliffs, surf breaks with names like a warning label, and the kecak fire dance at the temple as the sun went down. Watched surfers paddle into waves I would describe as "technically survivable."

## The Lombok Detour

The ferry to Lombok was slow, hot, and absolutely worth it:

- **Empty surf breaks** — paddled out at a spot with six people total, four of them local kids
- The Gili islands' no-cars policy: bicycles, horse carts, and silence
- A homestay owner who insisted on teaching us to make proper sambal, then critiqued ours mercilessly

> "Shortcuts" on a scooter in Ubud are a negotiation, not a route.

Came home sunburned, scooter-confident, and with a hard-to-shake habit of putting sambal on everything.
