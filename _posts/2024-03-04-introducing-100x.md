# Introducing 100x

In today's rapidly evolving technological landscape, staying ahead requires not just innovation but a profound understanding of how emerging technologies can be harnessed to drive real value. This is where 100x steps in, a new company on a mission to redefine the boundaries of engineering excellence. We are excited to introduce our range of engineering-related services designed to propel your business into the future. Our offerings are not just services; they are a partnership towards achieving exponential growth, leveraging the power of AI, and fostering an environment of continuous learning and development.

Our journey began with a clear vision: to provide businesses with access to cutting-edge engineering expertise without the overhead of full-time executive roles. Our Fractional Chief AI Officer and Fractional CTO services embody this philosophy, offering strategic leadership in AI and technology to navigate today's complex landscape. Whether it's integrating AI into your operations, developing AI-driven products, or steering your tech initiatives to align with your overall business strategy, our experts are here to guide you every step of the way.

In the realm of AI Engineering, we stand unparalleled. Our services span from selecting the right models to creating robust data pipelines and developing custom models tailored to your needs. We leverage Large Language Models (LLMs), Large Vision Models (LVMs), and the latest in Image & Video Generation systems to enhance your products and services. With our expertise in Langchain, LlamaIndex, and more, we ensure your AI initiatives are built on a solid foundation, poised to deliver unparalleled innovation and value.

But our commitment doesn't stop at technological prowess. At 100x, we believe in the power of open-source collaboration. We contribute to and maintain several open-source libraries under the MIT license, empowering developers and businesses alike to streamline AI development and deployment. Our libraries, including Typed Template and Typed LLM, are just the beginning of our contribution to a future where technology is accessible, reliable, and community-driven.

Understanding the importance of due diligence in the tech space, we offer comprehensive Technology Due Diligence services. From evaluating founding teams' skills to conducting technical product reviews and assessing compliance with industry standards like HIPAA and SOC2, our services provide the insights you need to proceed with confidence. We ensure that your investments are grounded in a thorough understanding of technological viability and security posture.

Moreover, we recognize that the core of any successful organization is its people. Our Mentoring services are designed to develop emerging leaders and maximize individual contributions, fostering a culture of growth, innovation, diversity, and inclusion. By applying the GROW Model of Mentoring, we help companies retain top talent and equip teams to face the challenges of tomorrow.

As we launch 100x, we invite you to explore the potential of a partnership that goes beyond the conventional. Visit [our contact page](https://www.100-x.ai/contact) to schedule time to discuss your project ideas with us. Let's embark on a journey of transformation, innovation, and exponential growth together. And if you know someone who could benefit from our services, we encourage you to share our offerings with them. Together, we can redefine the future of engineering, one project at a time.
