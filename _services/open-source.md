As part of our commitment to the AI community, we contribute to and maintain several open source libraries that empower developers and businesses alike. 
These tools are designed to streamline AI development and deployment, ensuring you can focus on providing value to your customers.

## Libraries

All of our libraries are open source under the MIT license, and we welcome contributions from the community.
Our current projects include:

- **Typed Template:** Pydantic String Templates for cleaner, more reliable code.
- **Typed LLM:** Extensible Pydantic LLM Primitives, enabling more efficient model integration.
- **Syncom:** Advanced LLM Data Pipelines (Coming Soon), providing the backbone for your AI projects.
