Make informed decisions with comprehensive due diligence reviews. 
From evaluating the skills of founding teams to conducting technical product reviews and assessing compliance with industry standards like HIPAA, SOC2, and PCI, our services provide the insights you need to proceed with confidence.

## Founding Team Skills Assessment

Our Technology Due Diligence service begins with a comprehensive Founder Skill Evaluation, assessing the leadership's technical acumen, vision, and ability to execute. 
We dive into the founders' backgrounds, track records, and leadership styles to understand how their capabilities can drive the company's success. 
This evaluation provides investors with insights into the founders' strengths and areas for development, ensuring the leadership team has the right mix of skills to navigate the challenges of scaling their technology.

## Technical Product Review

In the Technical Product Review phase, we meticulously analyze the company's core products from a technical perspective. 
This includes evaluating the architecture, scalability, and technological stack against industry standards and future requirements. We assess product-market fit, innovation, and the technology's ability to adapt to changing market demands. 
Our goal is to provide a clear picture of the product's technical viability and its potential for growth.

## Information Security Evaluation

Our InfoSec Evaluation is designed to scrutinize the company's information security posture, identifying potential vulnerabilities and assessing risk management strategies. 
We examine the company's adherence to best practices in cybersecurity, data protection policies, and incident response capabilities. 
This critical analysis ensures that the company's technology infrastructure is secure and resilient against emerging threats, safeguarding valuable data and intellectual property.

## Compliance Assessment

The Compliance Review segment of our service focuses on evaluating the company's adherence to relevant legal and regulatory standards. 
We cover a broad spectrum of compliance areas, including data privacy (GDPR, CCPA), industry-specific regulations (HIPAA for healthcare, PCI DSS for payment processing), and other global standards. 
Our comprehensive review ensures that the company is not only compliant with current regulations but is also prepared for future legislative changes, minimizing legal risks and fostering trust among users and investors.
